A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — a dark lord of Aldermere who has read the hero's prophecy in all seven copies and offers the whole damp kingdom away, keeping only the library and the good chair. The tiredness lives in the pauses and deflections, never in a plain statement of weariness. The program writes the monologue (100 words, 539 characters, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.
